What's inside (54 pages, with clickable bookmarks)

  • Whelping chart: breeding and progesterone record, due date tables for every breeding date of the year (window from the first breeding and from the LH surge), pre-whelping temperature log in °F and °C, whelping day timeline, puppy birth record
  • Puppy weight chart: daily litter log for days 0-13, weekly log for weeks 2-12, single puppy chart, growth graph, all in ounces and grams
  • Litter record book: litter summary, 10 puppy pages, placement list

  • Ounces, pounds and grams conversion tables

Where the numbers come from

Gestation ranges and the pre-whelping temperature drop in the whelping chart are taken from the Merck Veterinary Manual, "Whelping and Queening in Bitches and Queens" (reviewed June 2025), and cited inside the file. No vaccination schedule, no dosages and no target weights: you record what your vet advises.
