Why I make these

For years my days ran at two speeds: nothing at all, or everything at once. Planners never stuck. They wanted twelve goals and a color-coded week, and by Wednesday I had stopped opening them.

What worked was smaller. One thing that matters today. Three boxes instead of thirty. A timer set to fifteen minutes, and permission to stop when it rings. I started making my own pages that way, then making copies for friends with ADHD who saw them on my desk and asked for one.

FogliPronti means "ready-made pages" in Italian, which is where I design them. I test every page on my own week before it goes in the shop. I use AI tools to help build and check the files, and every listing says so.
