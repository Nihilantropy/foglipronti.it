What you get

Money talks go better with one number on the table. Each of you logs what you paid, and the sheet does the splitting: rent, groceries, the dinner you covered last Friday. At the end of the month it says who owes whom and how much, and a shared dashboard tracks your goals together.

What's inside (8 tabs)

  • Start here: setup in 5 steps, how to open it in Google Sheets
  • Setup: both names, currency, year, split method, your own shared and personal categories
  • Income: 12 months of take-home pay for each partner; each one's share is calculated for you
  • Shared Expenses: 600 rows with category, amount, paid by (either of you or the joint account) and an optional split per expense (50/50 or one person only)
  • Personal Expenses: each partner's own spending, kept out of the shared pot
  • Settle Up: month by month, what each of you paid, each fair share, WHO OWES WHOM and how much, what was paid back and what's still open
  • Joint & Goals: joint accounts with monthly contributions and up to 8 couple goals (wedding, house, travel) with progress bars and the month you'll get there
  • Dashboard: combined income, shared and personal spending, left over, balance between you, charts (shared spending by category, who paid the shared costs, income vs spending, goals)

How it works

  1. Type both names and choose your split: 50/50, by income (each pays in proportion to what they earn, recalculated every month) or a custom %.
  2. Log shared costs with who paid. Costs paid from a joint account are already shared.
  3. Open Settle Up: one line per month tells you who owes whom. Pay it back, type it in, done.
