What you get

I have ADHD, and for years "clean the kitchen" meant standing in the kitchen with no idea where to start. These lists are how I got out of that. Every task has its minutes printed next to it, most take 2 to 5, and every page holds only a few things. The rule is simple: set a 15-minute timer and stop when it rings. Skip a day and nothing piles up, because Tuesday's list just comes back next Tuesday.

I made the first pages for my own home, then for friends with ADHD who saw them on the fridge and asked for a copy. This is the full set, with every room in its own color.

How to use

  1. Pick one page: the daily reset or the room that bugs you most.
  2. Set a 15-minute timer and stop when it rings.
  3. Tick the box. Print a fresh copy each week, or fill it in on screen.
